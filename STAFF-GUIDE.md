# SK-image — staff guide

SK-image is where you add photos of our parts and add parts that aren't in BUSY. It works on your phone's browser
(Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone). Photos you take here go **straight to the website — they are not saved in your
phone's gallery**.

Your manager gives you the address and your password. The address ends in **/sk-image**.

---

## Log in
1. Open the SK-image address on your phone.
2. Type your **email** and **password**.
3. Tap **Sign in**.

Tip: add it to your home screen (Chrome: ⋮ → *Add to Home screen*; Safari: Share → *Add to Home Screen*).

## Log out
1. Tap **Logout** at the top.

---

## Find a product
1. Tap **Photos** at the top.
2. Type part of the **name** or the **item code (SKU)** in the big search box. Results appear as you type.
3. Use the buttons under the search box to narrow it:
   - **No photo** — parts that still need photos (start here)
   - **Has photo** · **BUSY** (from billing) · **Manual** (added here) · **All**
4. Tap the product.

The line at the top tells you how many products have photos.

## Take photos
1. Open the product and tap **Add photo**.
2. Tap **Take photo**. The first time, your phone asks to use the camera — tap **Allow**.
3. Point at the part and tap the big **round button**. The screen flashes and the phone buzzes.
4. Take more photos from other sides (up to **8 per product**). Each one uploads by itself.
5. Watch the small pictures at the bottom:
   - spinning = uploading · **✓** = done · **red ↻** = failed → tap it to try again (or **×** to throw it away)
6. Tap **Done** when finished. Wait for all ✓ before leaving the page.

**Good photos:** plain light background, part fills the frame, no shadow over it, label/code visible if there is one.
The round button at the bottom right switches between back and front camera.

**"Camera unavailable"?** Follow the message on screen to allow the camera, or tap **Use the phone camera instead**.

## Upload photos already on your phone
1. Open the product → **Add photo** → **Upload from phone**.
2. Pick one or more photos (JPG, PNG or WebP, under 10 MB each).

## Choose the main photo, reorder, delete
Under each photo:
- **☆ / ★** — tap the star to make it the **cover** (shown in the product list on the website). The first photo you add
  is the cover automatically.
- **← / →** — move the photo earlier or later.
- **Bin** — delete the photo. You'll be asked to confirm. This can't be undone.

Changes show on the website straight away.

---

## Add a product that isn't in BUSY
1. Tap **Add Product** at the top.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name** (required)
   - **SKU** — the item code shown to customers (required, must be unique)
   - **Category** — or leave *Auto* to guess from the name
   - **Price (₹)** — leave empty for "Price on request"
   - **Stock** — a whole number; 0 or empty shows "Check availability"
   - **Description** — optional
   - **Active** — on = shown on the website
3. Tap **Save and add photos**. You go straight to its photo screen.

## Edit a product
1. **Add Product** → scroll to **Manual products**.
2. Tap **Edit** next to it, change what you need, tap **Save and manage photos**.

## Hide a product (disable)
1. **Add Product** → **Manual products** → tap **Disable**.
It disappears from the website but nothing is lost. Tap **Enable** to bring it back.

## Delete a product permanently
1. **Add Product** → **Manual products** → tap **Delete** → confirm.
This removes the product **and all its photos** for good. If you might need it again, use **Disable** instead.

BUSY products can't be edited or deleted here — change them in BUSY. You can only add photos to them.

---

## Problems?
- **Wrong email or password** — check with your manager; they can reset it.
- **Upload keeps failing** — check your internet; failed photos retry by themselves when the connection is back.
- **"Photo limit reached (8)"** — delete one first.
- **Sync is late** badge on the Photos page — tell your manager (stock/prices from BUSY aren't updating).
